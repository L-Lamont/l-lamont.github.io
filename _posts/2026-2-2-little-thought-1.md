Sometimes you need a kick in the nuts to see how wrong you have been. Shocks to 
the system allow you to see you what you have been doing wrong. It makes it far 
easier to get perspective on your issues when they are presented to you in such 
a convinient form. It makes it difficult to avoid them. Well you can't avoid 
them and actually continue to be productive.
