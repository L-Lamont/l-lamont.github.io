# Reminder: Remember to Avoid the Bullshit

The engineers behind social media companies have weaponised basic biology. They 
prey on thousands of years of evolution to keep us distracted and engaged. 
During social media use it is so hard to acknowledge the negative 
consequences and not dismiss them. One more I say to myself again and again. If 
we know this going in why do we still fall for it though? Am I not able to 
control myself better than a machine can manipulate me? Sometimes I can, 
sometimes I can't. That is scary. Occasionally I have enough awareness to stop 
and see how this isn't serving me, but it is hard to remember that lesson.

This reminder is me trying to remember it. There are 2 thoughts that I want to 
keep front of mind and are generally useful.
- "You have never felt better about yourself after scrolling or watching 
  something you shouldn't."
- "What would you do if the people you respect the most were watching you right 
  now?"
