Are we what we repeatedly do? My day to day life dictates a lot of what I 
do but doesn't necessarily dictate how I experience it. A more holistic 
wording would be "We are what we repeatedly experience" this allows for the 
personal interpretation of events. 2 people going through the same events can 
have wildly different outlooks on life due to their interpretations.

If we take it as we are what we repeatedly experience there are now 2 clear 
things that affect our lives. What we do and how we interpret what we do. Sadly 
my education in fields that help you understand 
these sorts of things is lacking. It wasn't until reading Poor Charlie's 
Almanack that I even considered the benefits of learning about Psychology. Even
then I am only implementing its use after my 3rd or 4th time rereading it 
(I really think it's a good book). 

I am going to attempt to use the psychological tendencies outlined in Poor 
Charlie's Almanack to create Lollapalooza effects on my habits and my 
interpretation of them.
