Admit you don't want to be happy. You are actively putting constructs in place 
which are keeping you from being happy. 5 minutes off task only wastes a day if 
you let it. You control the fail criteria. You don't have to take it so 
seriously. Being able to laugh and smile through life seems a lot more pleasant 
than the alternative so put some effort into it. Even during WWII Churchill 
sung to himself, watched movies, rolled in the surf. If he can take the time to 
enjoy himself then, you can now.
