A dot point summary of my first meditation retreat and what I have taken from 
it.

- Kill your heros
  - Henry Shukman is a great teacher and someone who I listen to most days but 
    he is just a guy... silly to expect anything else but its comforting to see 
    the people we can sometimes put above ourselves are just people.
- When is the last time you sat in a room for 6 hours with ~100 people and you 
  didn't know anyone, weren't in typical age demographic, didn't share most 
  interests with people and had different political beliefs as people?
  - It was an interesting experience in getting outside of echo chambers and 
    opened my eyes to more opinions and beliefs
- It is not about not feeling anxious, nervous, scared, whatever its about not 
  minding that you are feeling them. Realising thats its your reaction to the 
  emotion that is important not whether or not you have the emotion.
- A point that confused me was when someone mentioned that they were frustrated 
  with world and the wars going on and that effected his meditation. I think 
  that the wars at the moment are bad but I also realise that there isn't 
  much I am willing to do about them so I shouldn't let them effect me. Is the
  guy who is getting frustrated by it and me having the same effect on the 
  issues (probably) am I far less impacted by it almost definitely.
  - At what point is my attitude not good though, what is the point where you 
    can actually make a difference or when should you stop letting it slide 
    because it doesn't impact you and you don't think you can change it?
- There is no destination or path when you look forward, when you look back you 
  might be able to put it together but you shouldn't be looking for one going 
  forward. Follow what feels right, it isn't going to be the same for everyone.
- It was eye opening to see how shy I was and how I lacked confidence when I 
  having multiple people actively listening to me. I had a clear aversion to 
  being the center of attention. This also extended to self conciousness when 
  just moving my body doing the warmup stretches.
  - Why do I still think people are judging me and why do I care if they are.
