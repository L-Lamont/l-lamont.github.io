Most of the time I do something stupid I know it is stupid and do it anyway... 
It does not require magic to prevent this it requires self control and generally 
a willingness to do something that is harder in the moment. Hopefully we can 
make that choice because the harder thing is more interesting and fun to do, 
but this is not always the case and we need to do the hard thing anyway.
